FISHVISID_VISION_ENGINE

這是一套讓魚類辨識回歸「拍照即知」的行動端系統。使用者透過手機相機拍攝或匯入照片，即可自動分類 魚種，並串接資料庫即時顯示俗名、營養價值與市場交易行情，解決傳統市場與超市選購海鮮時難以辨識魚種與行情的痛點。A mobile system that brings fish identification back to "just take a photo." Users capture or import a photo with their phone camera to automatically classify the fish species, connecting to a database to show the common name, nutritional value, and live market pricing in real time — solving the pain point of not being able to identify seafood species or prices at traditional markets and supermarkets.

🐟 核心技術與架構：Core Technology & Architecture:

  • 深度學習模型： 使用 TensorFlow / Keras 搭建 CNN 架構（Rescaling、Conv2D、MaxPooling2D、Flatten、Dense），進行圖像特徵萃取與訓練。
  • Deep Learning Model: Built a CNN architecture (Rescaling, Conv2D, MaxPooling2D, Flatten, Dense) with TensorFlow / Keras for image feature extraction and training.
  • 邊緣端部署： 透過 TensorFlow Lite (TFLite) 進行模型輕量化與轉換，直接部署至 Android 行動裝置進行離線推論。
  • Edge Deployment: Converted and lightened the model with TensorFlow Lite (TFLite) for direct deployment on Android devices for offline inference.
  • 資料工程與前處理： 自建跨季節魚類影像資料集並進行影像去背處理，降低環境雜訊干擾以提升辨識穩定度；整合政府農業交易公開數據進行行情加權分析。
  • Data Engineering & Preprocessing: Built a custom cross-season fish image dataset and applied background removal to reduce environmental noise and improve recognition stability; integrated open government agricultural trading data for weighted price analysis.
  • 前端與系統整合： 採用 Android Studio 開發互動介面，結合手機相機與本機圖庫模組，實現一鍵辨識流程。
  • Frontend & System Integration: Developed an interactive UI with Android Studio, combining the phone camera and local gallery modules into a one-tap recognition flow.

QUBO_MOSAIC_THESIS

本碩士論文致力於解決超大型組合最佳化問題中的「維度詛咒（Curse of Dimensionality）」。在生成完全不重複的相片馬賽克時，若將 35,000 張候選圖塊精準映射至 3,200 個目標方塊，會產生上億個二元變數，直接癱瘓傳統運算硬體。本研究提出一套混合式演算法，結合主成分分析與量子啟發退火技術，成功突破運算瓶頸，實現極致的視覺還原度。This master's thesis tackles the "curse of dimensionality" in extremely large-scale combinatorial optimization problems. When generating a completely non-repeating photo mosaic by precisely mapping 35,000 candidate tiles to 3,200 target blocks, the problem produces hundreds of millions of binary variables that cripple conventional computing hardware. This research proposes a hybrid algorithm combining Principal Component Analysis with quantum-inspired annealing to break through this computational bottleneck and achieve extreme visual fidelity.

⚛️ 核心技術與架構：Core Technology & Architecture:

  • PCA 遞迴降維空間切割： 將特徵資料轉換至 CIELAB 色彩空間，提取具最大變異數的第一主成分（PC1）進行遞迴對半切割，將龐大的搜尋空間降維成硬體可負荷的小型子問題。
  • Recursive PCA Space Partitioning: Converts feature data into CIELAB color space and extracts the first principal component (PC1, the axis of maximum variance) to recursively bisect the space, reducing the massive search space into small, hardware-feasible subproblems.
  • QUBO 模型建構： 將圖塊配對任務公式化為二次無約束二元最佳化（QUBO）模型，設計精確的懲罰項（Penalty）以嚴格確保「一方塊一圖塊」且「圖塊絕對不重複」的約束條件。
  • QUBO Model Formulation: Formulates the tile-matching task as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) model, with precisely designed penalty terms to strictly enforce "one tile per block" and "zero duplicate tiles" constraints.
  • 硬體求解與效能評估： 導入 Fujitsu Digital Annealer (DA) 與 Compal GPU Annealer (CGA) 進行求解。相較於傳統模擬退火（SA）容易陷入局部極小值並產生視覺瑕疵，DA 在子問題規模為 15 時展現最佳的色彩保真度（Lowest Delta E），並將執行時間穩定控制在 1,000-3,000 秒內。
  • Hardware Solving & Performance Evaluation: Solved with the Fujitsu Digital Annealer (DA) and Compal GPU Annealer (CGA). Compared to traditional Simulated Annealing (SA), which is prone to local minima and visible artifacts, the DA achieved the best color fidelity (lowest Delta E) at a subproblem size of 15, with runtime stably held between 1,000–3,000 seconds.
  • 學術認可： 此研究成果已入選 IEEE QCE26 國際量子運算會議海報發表。
  • Academic Recognition: This research has been accepted for poster presentation at the IEEE QCE26 international quantum computing conference.
